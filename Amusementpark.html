Amusement Park Challenge

Can you complete every ride before the park closes?

Challenge Rules

Visit all the attractions and collect a stamp at each one. Complete every ride to earn the "Ultimate Explorer" badge!

Don't forget to follow the SRI before entering any ride.

Height Requirement: Guests must be at least 130 cm tall for the Extreme Coaster.

Your Mission

  • Ride the Roller Coaster
  • Spin on the Ferris Wheel
  • Race on the Go-Karts
  • Explore the Pirate Ship
  • Escape the Haunted House

Good luck!
