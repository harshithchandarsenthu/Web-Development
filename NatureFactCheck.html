Nature


Here are 5 facts about nature:


Octopuses have 3 hearts:

Two hearts pump blood to the gills, while the third pumps blood to the rest of the body.

Trees can communicate with eachother:

Through underground fungal networks (sometimes called the "wood wide web"), trees can share nutrients and even send warning signals about pests.

Bananas are technically berries, but strawberries aren't:

In botanical terms, bananas meet the definition of a berry, while strawberries are classified as aggregate fruits.

Penguins:

Penguins propose to their mates by offering a smooth pebble.

Sunflowers can help clean polluted soil:

IThey absorb harmful substances like lead and arsenic from the ground, a process called phytoremediation.
